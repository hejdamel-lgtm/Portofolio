04 / WORKING TOGETHER

Clear terms.
Better creative.

Everything to know
before we begin.

01 Meetings & communication ＋

All meetings are held online, with a maximum of two meetings per project. Project communication takes place only through the channel agreed upon at the start.

If no feedback is received from the client for 14 days, the project will be considered complete.

02 Client assets & delivery ＋

The client must supply all required assets by the agreed deadline, including logos, photos or videos, product information, promotions, price lists and any other materials required by the brief.

All project assets and deliverables are shared through Google Drive, with content information and coordination managed through Google Sheets.

03 Payment & cancellation ＋

Full payment is required upfront before work begins. Payments are non-refundable if the client cancels the project.

04 Revisions & changes in scope ＋

One revision round is included. All revisions must follow the original, agreed brief.

A change of concept is treated as new work and will incur an additional fee. Requests to change content that has already been approved count as additional revisions and are subject to an extra charge.

05 Approvals, timelines & rush requests ＋

The client must provide approval within 1–2 business days. Late approvals will shift the posting timeline accordingly.

Urgent requests or work outside the agreed timeline may incur a rush fee, including requests with a turnaround of less than 24 hours.

06 Final content & information accuracy ＋

Once approved by the client, content is considered final. The client is responsible for the accuracy of the information and materials they supply, including any errors originating from those materials.
